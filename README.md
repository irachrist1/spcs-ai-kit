# SPCS AI kit

This is how I use AI agents to clean my inbox, send email from our domain, hunt for work and write proposals. I packaged it so Claude can teach you to do the same, on your own accounts.

You don't need to be technical. You paste one prompt and Claude takes it from there. It sets up your computer with you, explains each new idea in plain words, and goes one small step at a time. Every lesson ends with something working on your own accounts.

## Start

1. Open the Claude app on your computer.
2. Click **Code** at the top. Not Chat, not Cowork.
3. Pick any folder when it asks. Documents is fine.
4. Paste this and press Enter.

```
I'm new to AI agents. My co-founder Tonny made a kit that teaches me step by step, at https://github.com/irachrist1/spcs-ai-kit
Download it into spcs-ai/kit in my home folder (GitHub's zip download works, no git needed), then read START.md in it and follow it.
If you can't run commands on this computer, I'm in the wrong place. Tell me how to open the Code tab in the Claude desktop app, and stop there.
Teach me like a patient teacher. One small step at a time, in plain words, and wait for me before moving on.
Never send, post, pay, delete or share anything without my yes.
```

Claude asks before it does anything on your computer. A box pops up, you read the line, and you click Allow. You need a paid Claude plan for the Code tab.

## Coming back

Open the **Code** tab, choose the `spcs-ai` folder in your home folder, and say "let's continue". Claude picks up where you stopped.
