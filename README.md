# SPCS AI kit

This is how I use AI agents to run my week, hunt for work, write proposals, send email from our domain and make 3D pitches. I packaged it so you can do the same with your own accounts.

You don't need to be technical. Claude does the technical parts and explains what it's doing as it goes. Each lesson ends with something real on your accounts, not a demo.

## How to start

Open the Claude desktop app, click **Code** at the top (not Chat or Cowork), pick any folder and paste this. A paid plan is needed.

```
I'm new to AI agents. My co-founder Tonny packaged his AI setup for me at github.com/irachrist1/spcs-ai-kit.
Read START.md there and follow it. It works on Mac or Windows.
Do everything technical yourself, installs and accounts included, and teach me step by step in plain words.
Never send, post, pay, delete or share anything for me without asking me first.
```

That's it. Claude takes it from there.

To come back later, open the **Code** tab, choose the `spcs-ai` folder in your home folder, and say "let's continue".
