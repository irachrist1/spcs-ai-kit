# Lesson 1: Your first agent

**What you'll have at the end:** Claude connected to your email, calendar and Drive, and your inbox cleaned, with routine mail archived, junk filtered and replies drafted for what needs you.

**Time:** about 45 minutes.

## Why this lesson

An agent is only as useful as what it can reach. A chatbot answers questions. An agent connected to your accounts does the work. Your inbox is the fastest way to feel the difference.

## Steps (for the agent)

### 1. Connect the accounts

Walk your person through Claude's settings, under **Connectors**. Connect, in this order:

1. Their work email (Gmail or Outlook, from the progress file).
2. Their calendar.
3. Google Drive or OneDrive, wherever their documents live.

Explain in one line what a connector is. It's a door that lets Claude read and act in that app, which they can close at any time from the same screen.

Then **check which account each connector is really signed in to.** Search their mail for something only that inbox would have, and list one upcoming event. Connectors are often quietly signed in to the wrong account (a personal or old address). If one is wrong, disconnect it and connect the right one.

### 2. Read the inbox

Read the last 30 days of their inbox. Sort every thread into three groups:

- **Needs you:** a real person waiting on a reply or a decision.
- **Routine:** receipts, notifications, newsletters they actually read, things already handled.
- **Junk:** mail they should never see again (cold sales, daily promos, services they don't use).

Show them a short summary. The count in each group, the senders behind most of the routine and junk, and every "needs you" thread in one line each, most urgent first.

### 3. Your yes: the clean-up plan

Propose the plan:

- Archive the routine threads. Archiving keeps them searchable and can be undone. Never delete.
- For each junk sender, either unsubscribe (show the list first) or add a Gmail filter that skips the inbox.
- Draft a reply for each "needs you" thread, in their voice, following the writing rules in START.md.

Wait for their yes. They can drop any item.

### 4. Do it

- Archive the approved threads.
- Create the filters. If you can't create filters directly, open Gmail's filter settings in the browser and walk them through the clicks for the first one, then do the rest the same way.
- Save each reply as a **draft**. Don't send.

### 5. Check the result

Open the inbox again and confirm it holds only the "needs you" threads. List the drafts you saved.

### 6. Wrap up

Tell them, in three short lines:

- What they now have (a clean inbox, N filters, N drafts waiting).
- What they learned (connectors give the agent hands; checking which account is connected matters; drafts mean nothing leaves without their yes).
- What to try on their own this week: "Clean my inbox" once a day takes two minutes now.

Note the result in the progress file. Offer Lesson 2.

## If something goes wrong

- **No connectors screen:** they may be on a free plan, or an older app. Update the app and check the plan.
- **Outlook instead of Gmail:** the steps are the same. Use rules instead of filters.
- **Too many threads to read:** start with the last 7 days, then widen.
